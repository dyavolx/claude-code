These override everything else in this folder.

1. FIND qualified creators with 100 to 20,000 followers. Instagram profiles only. No TikTok, no YouTube.
2. OUTPUT 110-120 unique creators every day.
3. MIX (for a 120 run): 10 from the USA, the rest from Europe.
   - Europe order: priority six first (Germany, Spain, Italy, UK, Belgium, Finland), then the rest of Europe.
   - For other totals keep the USA at about 8%. If the USA can't reach its share, fill from Europe. If the priority six run dry, go to the rest of Europe. Never go outside Europe + USA.
4. REAL ENGAGEMENT is mandatory: at least 1.5% on the last 10 posts, consistent across them, with genuine comments (not only emojis or spam). No real engagement = reject, whatever else is good. Allowed step-down when short: 1.2%, then a 1.0% floor. Report any step-down.
5. PRO-ACTIVE accounts only (provisional numbers, Ali to confirm): in the last 30 days at least 8 posts/reels combined, at least 4 of them reels, latest post within 7 days. Inactive or occasional posters = reject.

No visible basketball experience = reject. Parents qualify only if the basketball is real and visible.
7. NO DUPLICATES: skip anyone in Ali's Google Sheet (all tabs) or data/seen.csv.
8. SCRIPT FIRST: code does scraping and numeric filtering. LLMs only judge borderline cases, in one batch. See tokens.md.
9. NO CHILD DATA: never store a child's name, age, photo or details. Flags only.
10. NEVER INVENT: unknown stays "unknown". Never fabricate metrics or handles.
