# Criteria: SilentBall Academy campaign (Ali's decisions, Oct 8)

## Who we want
Instagram creators in three lanes:
1. Basketball coaches / trainers / skills or shooting coaches / youth player-development
2. Parent creators with a real basketball or active-play link
3. Sports / basketball creators with relevant, repeatable practice content
Most important: the account is likely to drive affiliate sales (pro-active, real engagement, relevant audience). Product fit: quiet at-home practice, ball handling, shooting, drills, active-family play.

## Targets
- Followers: 100 to 20,000. A 100-follower account is fine if engagement is real and it is pro-active.
- Engagement: 1.5% minimum on the last 10 posts. Step down only when short: 1.2%, then 1.0% floor. State any change in the daily summary.
- Activity (mandatory, provisional): last 30 days at least 8 posts/reels with at least 4 reels, latest post within 7 days. Ali to confirm.
- Language: English first. Local-language accounts are fine.
- Ad rights: creator must be willing to grant them. Record "unknown / to ask"; never assume yes.

## Country mix (mandatory, see commands.md)
- USA: 10 per 120 creators.
- Europe: the rest. Priority six first (Germany, Spain, Italy, UK, Belgium, Finland), then the rest of Europe (France, Portugal, Austria, Ireland, Poland, Netherlands, etc.).
If country is unclear, write "unverified" and keep as hold. Never guess.

## Parent-lane check (inferred from Ali's sheet notes; Ali to confirm)
Ali's notes reject parents whose kids are "much less than 6" and ask for "some level of basketball for kids". For parent creators: kids visibly around 6+ or clear basketball/active-play content. Store only a yes/no flag, never a child's name, age or details.

## Hard gates (fail any one = reject)
1. Adult creator or a parent as the contact. Never a child's account.
2. Country is Europe or USA, and the mix quota allows it.
3. Followers 100-20,000.
4. Genuine audience (no obvious bought followers) and real engagement.
5. Pro-active: meets the activity rule.
6. Mandatory: real basketball experience visible in recent posts (playing, coaching, training, drills), so they can use and demonstrate the product. Active-play alone is not enough.
7. Not a competitor, no brand-safety concerns.
8. Not already in Ali's Google Sheet (any tab) or data/seen.csv.
Hold (not reject): basketball evidence unclear and needs a manual look, engagement hard to judge, country unverified.

## Soft preferences
Public contact route. Coaches with clear programs or camps. Clear repeatable format.

## Exclude
Minors, inactive, off-niche, duplicates, anyone Ali already contacted.
