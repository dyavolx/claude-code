# discovery-method.md

## Method evidenced by the sheet and prior chats

1. Discover public profiles with **SocialCrawl** when credits are available. The sheet notes also cite public profile/bio discovery and public sources/tools named previously: Agent-Reach, Scrapling, ScrapeGraphAI, Scout, Jina Reader, Modash, Flinque, Keepface, JoinBrands, Viralist, Jolygram, StarNgage, Collabstr, and creator/club/training websites.
2. Search Instagram first, across **USA plus the EU markets Germany, Spain, Italy, UK, Belgium, and Finland**, while accepting active TikTok/YouTube accounts where the campaign permits them. The sheet rows are overwhelmingly Instagram. Capture the public profile URL/handle, platform, niche, country, follower count, and lead date.
3. Pre-screen against the active follower band, geography, niche/product fit, public activity, and genuine engagement. For parent/family leads, record active-child/sports/family or basketball evidence. For coaches, record basketball coaching, shooting, skills, player-development, or youth-training evidence.
4. Verify with public profile/posts/reels and, when available, third-party public metrics. Observed note formats include SocialCrawl recent-post medians such as **14 likes / 0.5 comments**, **100 / 8**, **56 / 3**, and **51.5 / 3**; Flinque ER examples of **4.91%, 1.52%, 0.61%, 1.66%, 1.70%, 6.98%, 2.15%, 22.38%, and 1.10%**; Keepface examples of **3.2% and 6.5%**; and Modash **2.27%** with average Reel plays **15.7K**. These are observed examples, not a universal threshold.
5. Record qualification rationale and evidence URLs. The sheet’s approval notes commonly document recent dates, likes/comments, coaching credentials, commercial/contact pathways, and a product-fit explanation. Do not claim SilentBall use or endorsement unless separately evidenced.
6. Deduplicate against the live Sheet1/profile-link list, prior groups, current blocklists, and repeated URLs before merging. Keep outreach status separate from qualification.
7. Preserve an ad-rights field or review flag. The campaign overview requires willingness to grant ad rights, but the rows do not consistently record a yes/no answer.
